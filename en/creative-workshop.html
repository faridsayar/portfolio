Creative Kids' Workshop in East Oslo (Ages 5–9)

Formaa Creative Workshop is an engaging hands-on program in Trosterud, Oslo, designed for children aged 5–9 and created by Formaa AS. Sessions run for 90 minutes once a week for 8 weeks. The exact date and location will be shared directly with those who register their interest.

Our mission is to give children space to explore, build, and create with their hands. Through art, crafts, design, and simple construction projects, kids learn to develop ideas, solve problems, and experience the joy of making.

We aim to inspire a new generation of makers — children who dare to experiment, explore, and find unique solutions while building practical skills.

Why Formaa?

From a Real Design Studio to a Kids' Workshop

At Formaa AS, we design and build real products from initial idea to prototype — working hands-on with materials, forms, and tools every day. Our workshop was created by industrial designers and product developers, then thoughtfully adapted for children.

This isn't just a basic craft class. It's a structured workshop where kids experience the same creative mindset used in professional design — fueled by play, curiosity, and hands-on discovery.

Meet the team or explore our projects to see who's behind the studio.

Instructors

Who Teaches the Workshops?

Sessions are led by the Formaa team — industrial designers and product developers with deep hands-on experience in sketching, prototyping, and physical production.

We bring real-world expertise with professional tools and materials, adapting each task so children can tackle real challenges safely and with confidence.

Our focus isn't on flawless results. We want every child to feel empowered to experiment, learn from mistakes, and try again — exactly like in a real design studio.

Our Philosophy

Every product around us started as a simple idea.

Children possess a natural sense of curiosity and boundless imagination. When given the right materials, tools, and the freedom to experiment, they develop creativity, practical skills, and confidence in their own abilities.

At Formaa Creative Workshop, there are no single right answers. We empower children to explore, ask questions, and discover their own unique solutions.

Tools

We guide children to use real tools safely and confidently, teaching them proper techniques so they can bring their ideas to life.

  • Hammers, screwdrivers, and tape measures
  • Sandpaper, paintbrushes, and glue
  • Hand drills, rulers, and compasses
  • Try squares, marking gauges, and wrenches
  • Bolts, nuts, and small clamps

Creativity & Upcycling

An essential part of our workshop is showing children that everyday items can be given a second life. We encourage kids to collect interesting materials at home or in nature and bring them to special sessions — where we help turn those objects into brand-new creations.

  • Corks, cardboard boxes, and buttons
  • Wood scraps, pinecones, and stones
  • Fabric scraps, old toys, and containers
  • Glass jars, wire, wheels, and small mechanical parts

We build curiosity.

Building & Drawing with Kids: Example Projects

  • Build a personalized toolbox or a small car
  • Craft a wooden boat, bird feeder, or windmill
  • Create a treasure chest, bow and arrow, or fantasy creature
  • Sculpt clay figures and bake them
  • Assemble a simple fishing rod, airplane, or wooden clock
  • Construct mechanical toys to experiment with balance and movement
  • Design original inventions over multi-week projects

We guide children through a complete "project workflow" — covering planning, sketching, material selection, measuring, and execution. This teaches patience, long-term focus, and the joy of seeing an idea through to the end.

A Typical Session

No two days are identical; every session leaves plenty of room for kids' own ideas.

Welcome & Inspiration (10 mins)

Intro to the day's theme, group discussion, and sketching in their project books.

Exploration (15 mins)

Discovering new tools and materials through live demonstrations and hands-on trial.

Making Time (50 mins)

Kids choose materials, build, paint, sculpt, and solve challenges. The instructor provides personalized guidance.

Show & Tell (10 mins)

Children present their projects and share their creative thought process with the group.

Wrap-Up & Reward (5 mins)

Tidying up together, learning tool care, and receiving the day's sticker.

Safety First

Safety is our foundation — it gives children the freedom to explore with confidence. All activities are tailored to age and skill levels under direct supervision.

  • Gradual Tool Introduction: No child is rushed into using tools before they feel ready.
  • Small Group Sizes: Ensures personalized guidance and a controlled environment.
  • Strict Supervision: No heavy or dangerous machinery is used without one-on-one oversight.
  • Clear Routines: Structured protocols for handling, cleaning, and storing tools safely.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my child need to be "creative" to join?

Not at all — curiosity is all they need! Some kids love to draw, while others just want to build, and both are equally welcome. Our workshops are all about experimenting and trying new things, not having pre-existing skills.

What should my child wear?

Comfortable clothes that can get a little messy! Choose something you don't mind getting dusty or covered in glue. We provide aprons and all necessary protective gear.

What if my child doesn't like drawing, but loves building?

That is completely normal! The sketchbook is just a tool, not a strict requirement — many of our projects start directly with materials and building. Our instructors adapt the pace and teaching methods to match every child's unique interests.

Can I register interest for siblings of different ages?

Yes! You can fill out a separate form for each child or simply note in the form how many children you are considering. Our groups are designed for the 5–9 age range.
