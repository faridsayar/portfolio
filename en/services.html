Process

The full product development cycle can be described in six main phases, each with smaller steps. The process is often not linear, but spiral-shaped: in dialogue with the client we run iterations, offer variations and develop solutions.

1

Analysis

user research, market research, field study, mapping, requirements specification

2

Concept development

sketching, idea development, concept development, solution proposals, quick visualizations

3

Form development

detailed sketches, 3D model, form exploration, function analysis, material choice, mock-ups — see industrial design

4

Visualization

Quality stills, animation, images and video, use in context and surroundings

5

Prototype

Physical testing: ergonomics, form, functions, materials, design language, use situations. Read how to make a prototype

6

Validation

Confirm and validate choices, final CAD model, tolerances, production costing, production prep, technical drawings. See from invention to production

What we do

Workshops and production

Production in Norway

We are in dialogue with potential partners for production in Norway and actively building contacts for more varied production types — with local labour and Norwegian raw materials where possible, for shorter and safer transport routes and more sustainable production.

Production abroad

We work with companies that have their own production in China, long experience with shipping and logistics to Europe, and certified validation against relevant standards — from CE marking and production testing to quality control before shipment.

In parallel we are talking with several suppliers in Europe and Scandinavia, expanding capacity across materials and volumes — so we can find the right solution for prototypes, low-volume runs and larger series.
