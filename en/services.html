Process

Six phases — often iterative rather than strictly linear. We adapt the sequence to your risk, timeline, and manufacturing goals.

1

Analysis

Clarify users, constraints, market context, and success criteria.

2

Concept

Explore directions, compare options, and lock a direction worth building.

3

Form development

Shape, ergonomics, and detail that support use and manufacture.

4

Visualization

Renders and motion to communicate the product clearly.

5

Prototype

Physical models to test fit, feel, and assumptions before tooling.

6

Validation

Check against goals, refine, and prepare production-ready deliverables.
