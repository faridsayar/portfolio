FAQ

FAQ about product development

1. What does a design project cost?

Cost depends on complexity, scope and timeline. We set a clear plan with prioritized activities so you can start at the right level and scale further. See our pricing overview for a quick estimate.

2. How long does it take from idea to prototype?

A first prototype can often be developed in a few weeks, while a complete production path normally takes several phases. We clarify this at kickoff and often use prototyping and CAD modeling to reduce risk.

3. Do you only work with companies in Oslo?

No. We can meet and work with you in Oslo and nearby regions, and support the rest of Norway digitally with limited in-person sessions. Prototype shipping is possible but adds time and should be planned for.

4. Can you join a project that is already underway?

Yes. We can contribute as a specialist for parts of the journey or own a full phase — for example concept, design improvement, prototyping or CAD modeling.

5. What deliverables do we get along the way?

You get concrete deliverables: decision material, concepts, models, prototypes and recommendations, test results, technical drawings and a production plan. See examples on our project pages.

6. How do we start working together?

Send a short request via the form, or email hei@formaa.no. We start with an intro call, clarify goals and propose a practical first-phase setup. You can also read more about us first.

7. Do you work with hardware startups in Norway?

Yes. We regularly do product development for startups with physical products — from early idea to prototype and production prep. See our services.

8. Can you help me get an invention manufactured?

Yes, in most cases. We help structure product development, build a prototype and prepare technical documentation so you can move toward manufacturing. See our services overview.
