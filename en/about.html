Formaa AS is three designer friends who started a unique product development studio to help inventors, founders and startups bring strong product ideas to market.

We would like to get to know you.

What sets us apart is a personal approach. No funding yet? No problem. Get in touch, meet us, and we will give practical advice for your concrete idea. Visit the workshop or join a sketch session for human attention, tips and guidance at no cost — just bring your own lunch and a good mood. We also run kids courses in Oslo for ages 5–9 at Formaa Makerspace.

A bit about us

A creative, technical and hands-on team. Born in 1989 and 1990, we took very different paths into design. We grew up with the internet and digital tools, which makes us adaptive and flexible in today’s turbulence. We think differently, challenge each other in a good way, and solve problems with humor.

Our mission is to make cool products that actually solve something, feel good to use, stay sustainable, and make the world a little better.

Our competence

We studied at the Oslo School of Architecture and Design (AHO) and USN – University of South-Eastern Norway. That gives us a solid foundation in form, function and how products are actually made — not only how they look on screen.

We have worked as freelance and in-house designers for companies such as ReTyre, Memorium, RT, Fixa, Rolling Moto and Div.Pro — covering everything from visualization and CAD models to branding and production prep. We currently help the guitar startup Obseed prepare for series production and CAD modeling — and continue with them toward larger production and a marketing website.
