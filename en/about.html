Formaa AS

We would like to get to know you

We are a product development studio in Oslo for inventors, founders, and startups building physical products. Personal approach — whether you already have funding or are still validating the idea.

A bit about us

A creative, technical, and hands-on team. We took different paths into design, grew up with digital tools, and stay adaptive as products and manufacturing change. We challenge each other — and solve hard problems with humor.

Our mission is to build products that solve a real problem, feel good to use, and can be made responsibly.

What we bring

Background from AHO and USN — form, function, and how products are actually made. Experience across freelance and in-house work; today we push projects toward serial production when that is the goal.

What we deliver

Concept, industrial design, product development, CAD, branding, and packaging.

Prototyping & production

Prototypes, DFM thinking, drawings, and support with local or overseas manufacturing.

With partners

Deeper technical work when needed — analysis, CAM, materials, and electronics via trusted partners.
