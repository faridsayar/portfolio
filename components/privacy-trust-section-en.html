Privacy and confidentiality

Protecting ideas and trade secrets

Formaa is an independent design studio. We build trust through clear boundaries — not by collecting data on the website. This is how we handle privacy, information you share, and confidentiality in client work.

No storage or sharing of visitor data

We do not collect, store or sell data about visits to formaa.no, and we do not share such information with third parties. Information you send via the contact form is used only to answer your inquiry — not for profiling or marketing.

Independent design studio

We are an independent industrial and product design studio without a corporate structure that would require sharing project information across portfolios. Your projects and decisions stay with us and with you.

Confidentiality under Norwegian law

Project information, sketches and trade secrets are handled with professional confidentiality in line with Norwegian law, including rules on trade secrets and ordinary duty of confidentiality in client relationships. A written NDA can be agreed when the project requires it.

Trademarks and consumer guidelines

We follow applicable rules for trademarks, copyright and rights, and align with consumer guidelines and good business practice in marketing and client relationships.

Portfolio only with your consent

We do not publish finished work, work in progress or other project-related information — online or in presentations — unless you explicitly approve what may be shown and when it may appear in our portfolio.
