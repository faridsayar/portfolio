Privacy & Confidentiality

Protecting Ideas & Trade Secrets

Formaa is an independent design studio. We build trust through clear, transparent boundaries — not by tracking website visitors. Here is how we handle privacy, user-submitted information, and client confidentiality.

No Tracking or Data Sharing

We do not collect, store, or sell data regarding visits to formaa.no, nor do we share visitor information with third parties. Details sent via our contact form are strictly used to respond to your inquiry — never for profiling or marketing.

Independent Design Studio

We operate as an independent industrial and product design studio with no corporate entanglements that would force us to share project information. Your projects, ideas, and decisions stay strictly between you and us.

Confidentiality Under Norwegian Law

Project files, sketches, and trade secrets are protected under strict professional confidentiality in compliance with Norwegian law (including the Trade Secrets Act and standard duty of confidentiality in commercial relationships). A formal Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) can be executed whenever required.

IP & Consumer Practice

We strictly adhere to applicable regulations governing trademarks, copyrights, and intellectual property, operating in full compliance with Norwegian consumer protection guidelines and ethical business practices.

Portfolio Publication Only with Explicit Consent

We never publish finished work, work in progress, or project details — whether online or in pitch decks — without your explicit prior approval specifying what may be shown and when.
